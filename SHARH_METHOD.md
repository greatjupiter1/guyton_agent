# روش شرح — The Sharh Method (Guyton lessons)

Ordered by Jupiter, 2026-11-21. This is the standing method for the شرح
(explanation) of every Guyton page this agent teaches. It is enforced in the
GEMINI_PROMPT of `build_lesson.py` (the `explanation_fa` field).

## ایدهٔ اصلی — The core idea

شرح هر صفحه باید همان‌طور نوشته شود که بهترین استادان فیزیولوژی جهان درس
می‌دهند: ترکیبی از سنتِ تدریسِ ۱۰۰ منبع گردآوری‌شده در `SOURCES.md` —
جزوه‌های دانشگاهی بر پایهٔ گایتون، TeachMePhysiology، OpenStax،
LibreTexts، Ninja Nerd، Dr Najeeb، Armando Hasudungan، Medical Gateway
و بقیه. نه خلاصهٔ خشکِ متن کتاب، بلکه یک درسِ کامل، انسانی و روان.

The explanation of each page is written the way the world's best physiology
teachers teach — the combined tradition of the 100 sources in `SOURCES.md`.
Not a dry summary of the book: a complete, human, fluent lesson.

## قوانین — The rules

1. **یک درسِ منسجم (One coherent lesson).** The شرح is a single connected
   lesson, never disconnected notes. Each section links back to the
   previous one and points forward to the next; the whole page reads as
   one story.
2. **پاراگراف‌به‌پاراگراف (Paragraph by paragraph).** EVERY paragraph of
   the page gets its OWN `###` section, in the page's exact order. Never
   merge two paragraphs, never skip one. If a paragraph continues from the
   previous page, say so and complete its idea.
3. **کامل و دقیق (Complete and precise).** Every fact, number, thickness,
   percentage and name is *explained*, not repeated: what the number
   means, a familiar comparison, and why it matters physiologically.
   Every mechanism is broken into its small steps, in order.
4. **مثالِ روان برای هر پاراگراف (A fluent example for every paragraph).**
   Each section contains at least one concrete, vivid example from
   everyday life or the clinic, introduced with «مثال:» — first the
   analogy or living picture, then the exact mechanism it stands for.
5. **مکانیسم پیش از حفظ‌کردن (Mechanism before memory).** Always explain
   HOW and WHY before naming anything to memorise. End each section with
   one short line beginning «جمع‌بندی:» — the single idea the student
   must carry away from that paragraph.
6. **انسانی، گرم و روان (Human, warm, fluent).** Natural flowing Persian,
   a kind teacher speaking to one student; short clear sentences; a
   guiding question now and then («حالا فکر کن...»); every technical term
   explained at first use with the English term in parentheses.
7. **امانت (Fidelity).** Stay faithful to the page and to standard
   physiology. Never invent numbers, names or mechanisms the page does
   not support.
8. **مستند (Documented) — added by Jupiter, 2026-11-21.** EVERY section
   of the شرح ends with a sources line beginning «منابع این بخش:» that
   cites (a) this book — Guyton and Hall, 13th edition, with chapter and
   the printed page being taught — and (b) at least one supporting
   teaching source from `SOURCES.md` whose teaching that section's
   explanation or example follows, named exactly, with its chapter,
   section or page whenever genuinely known. NEVER invent a page number,
   chapter or link: if the exact page is not known, cite the chapter or
   topic title instead. Every claim in a section must be traceable to
   the book page or to one of its cited sources.
