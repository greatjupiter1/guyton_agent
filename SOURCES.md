emphasis on YouTube teachings and the universities of Europe, America and
Asia). The شرح of every Guyton lesson is written in the combined teaching
tradition of these sources — see `SHARH_METHOD.md`. The book page itself
(Guyton and Hall, 13th ed.) always remains the factual authority.
